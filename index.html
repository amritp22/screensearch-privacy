ScreenSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short

  • ScreenSearch works on your phone. Your screenshots and the text read from them stay on your device.
  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Who we are

ScreenSearch (“the app”) is an Android app that sorts and searches the screenshots on your phone. It is published by Amrit Pal Singh (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at amritpb.dev@gmail.com.

2. What the app accesses on your phone

  • Photos and media permission. The app asks for permission to read images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13 and newer, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions). It uses this only to find the screenshots in your Screenshots folder and read them so they can be sorted and searched.
  • The app does not edit, move, rename or delete your screenshots. Your original files are left as they are.
  • The app does not use your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

3. What the app stores on your device

To work quickly, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • the text it reads from each screenshot, so you can search it;
  • a reference to each screenshot (its identifier and location on your phone);
  • the category the app chose for each screenshot, such as Shopping, Payments, Travel or Food;
  • small preview images of your screenshots, kept in the app’s cache;
  • your custom sorting rules and your appearance choice (light, dark or night mode).

This information is protected by Android’s app sandbox, is not backed up by the app, and is never sent to us. Because screenshots can contain personal details such as payment confirmations or tickets, we want to be clear that this information stays on your device.

4. How your screenshots are processed

The app reads the text in your screenshots using Google’s ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device. According to Google’s ML Kit terms, the images and text being processed are handled fully on the device and are not sent to Google’s servers. The app then decides each screenshot’s category using rules and word lists that are built into the app, also on your device.

5. Technical information sent to Google by ML Kit

ML Kit also sends limited technical data to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. According to Google’s published disclosure, this can include device information (such as manufacturer, model and operating system version), app information (such as the app’s package name and version), performance and error information, and an identifier for the app installation that is not intended to identify you personally. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. It does not include your screenshots or the text read from them. We do not receive this data. For details, see Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We did not add any analytics or tracking tools of our own to the app.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can withdraw the photos and media permission at any time in your phone’s Settings. The app will then be unable to read your screenshots.
  • You can delete everything the app has stored by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we do not collect your data, we hold nothing about you on any server.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Please check it from time to time.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, email amritpb.dev@gmail.com.
